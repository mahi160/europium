# Office Service

Employees request refreshments and staff fulfill their orders.

## Language

**Requester**: The person who placed an order. Only the requester can cancel a Pending order; administrators cannot cancel orders.

**Assignee**: The staff member who accepted an order and is responsible for delivering it or returning it to the queue.

**Return to pending**: The assignee releases an Accepted order back into the queue without an assignee. The reason is optional.
